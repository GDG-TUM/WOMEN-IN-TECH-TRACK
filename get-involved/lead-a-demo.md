# 🎤 Lead a Demo

A demo is a short, hands-on moment where you show something and the audience tries it. Demos at the **cybersecurity basics** session (26 Oct) are led by women in the club.

## Pick one thing
Choose **one skill** the audience can use today. Examples for cybersecurity basics (suggestions, the demo team decides):
- Spot a scam message
- Turn on two-factor authentication
- Review app permissions on your phone
- What to do if you lose your phone

## Plan it (10 minutes of demo, 10 minutes of practice)
1. **Hook:** one real-life story or question (1 minute)
2. **Show it:** do the steps slowly on a screen (4 minutes)
3. **Try it:** everyone does it on their own phone or account (10 minutes)
4. **Wrap up:** one sentence to remember, and where to learn more (2 minutes)

## Rehearse
Run it once with a friend. Time it. Cut anything that is not essential.

## Safety rules
- Use **fictional accounts and sample data**, or your **own** accounts and devices
- **Never** show anyone's real password, code or private messages
- Nobody looks at anyone else's phone
- If you are unsure, ask the organisers

## Afterwards
- Add your steps to the [event recap](../events/2026-10-26-cybersecurity-basics.md) or the [showcase](../showcase/README.md)
- Tell us how it went with the [feedback form](https://github.com/GDG-TUM/women-in-tech/issues/new/choose)

## Nervous?
That is normal. Practise out loud, pair up with another demo leader, and remember that the audience wants you to do well.
