# 🤝 Get Involved

Everyone is welcome. If you want to do more than attend, here are the ways to be **in front**.

| Role | What it means | Related event | Guide |
|------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| **Participant** | Come, try, ask, join a team | Any Monday | [Getting Started](../GETTING-STARTED.md) |
| **Demo leader** | Run a short, hands-on demo | Cybersecurity basics (26 Oct) | [Lead a demo](lead-a-demo.md) |
| **Team lead** | Guide a small group to a result | Data science (9 Nov), build session (16 Nov), hackathon (23 Nov) | [Team lead guide](team-lead-guide.md) |
| **Session helper** | Help run a session | Intro to cloud computing (19 Oct) | Ask the Women in Tech Lead |
| **Study group host** | Keep a small group learning together | After the cloud session | Ask the Women in Tech Lead |
| **Spreader of the word** | Invite friends, share the posts | All | Share the [calendar](../calendar/README.md) |

## Volunteer
Use the [volunteer form](https://github.com/GDG-TUM/women-in-tech/issues/new/choose). It takes two minutes. Tell us how you would like to help and which events suit you.

## You do not need to be an expert
Demo leaders and team leads are supported. The aim is that women lead and **everyone** learns, including the leaders.
