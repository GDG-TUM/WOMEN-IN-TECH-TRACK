# 👩🏾‍💼 Team Lead Guide

At the data science session (9 Nov), the build session (16 Nov) and the hackathon (23 Nov), **women lead the teams**. This guide is for you.

## Your job
- **Keep the team on track** and on time
- **Make sure everyone is heard**, including quieter members
- **Share the work** so everyone gets hands-on time
- **Keep the scope small** so the team finishes something
- **Present** the result, or choose who will

## Before the session
- Read the event page and its notes
- Check what the team needs to bring
- Think of a simple plan for the time you have

## During the session
1. **Introduce everyone** and ask what each person wants to learn
2. **Agree the goal** in one sentence
3. **Split the work** into small tasks, with a mix of skills
4. **Check in** every 15 to 20 minutes
5. **Ask for help** early from the organisers or other teams
6. **Leave time to demo** what you have, even if it is small

## For mixed teams (developers and designers)
- Developers build, designers shape the look and graphics, and everyone reviews together
- Agree the first screen or page and make it work before adding more

## For data teams
- Use **public, open data** with no personal information
- Write down what question you are asking before you start
- Share what you found in plain language, not only code

## After the session
- Share a recap or photos in the [showcase](../showcase/README.md)
- Tell us what worked with the [feedback form](https://github.com/GDG-TUM/women-in-tech/issues/new/choose)
- Thank your team

## You are not alone
Organisers and other team leads are there to help. Ask.
