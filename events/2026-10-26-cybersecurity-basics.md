# Cybersecurity basics

**Date:** Mon 26 Oct 2026 · **Track:** Cybersecurity

**Time and venue:** announced before the day. **[TO CONFIRM]** (dates may shift if the university changes its calendar)

## What to expect
How to keep your accounts and phone safe. The demos will be done by the women in the club.

## Where women are in front
The women in the club do the demos.

## Come prepared
- Bring your **phone**
- Bring any accounts you want to secure, but **never share passwords or codes** with anyone

## Notes
**Safety:** demos use fictional accounts and sample data, or your **own** accounts and devices. Nobody looks at anyone else's phone. Suggested demo ideas (the demo team decides): spotting a scam message, turning on two-factor authentication, reviewing app permissions, and what to do if you lose your phone.

## 🏁 After the event
- [ ] Finish securing your own accounts and phone
- [ ] Help a friend or family member do the same
- [ ] Explore the [Cybersecurity Track](https://github.com/GDG-TUM/cybersecurity-track)
