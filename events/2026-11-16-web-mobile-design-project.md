**Date:** Mon 16 Nov 2026 · **Track:** Web dev, mobile, design

## What to expect
Developers build, designers do the graphics and the look of the app. Women lead the teams.

## Where women are in front
Women lead the teams, and developers and designers work side by side.

## Come prepared
- A laptop for developers
- Design tools or sketches for designers (to be confirmed)
- Bring an idea, or pick one on the day
