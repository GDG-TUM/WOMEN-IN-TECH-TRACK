# 💜 About Women in Tech

**Women in Tech** at GDG on Campus TUM is a Monday event series for Semester 1 (October to November 2026). Every Monday has something for women in tech, and **each one is linked to a track we already have in the club**.

Everyone is welcome, but we want **the women to be the ones in front**.

## What "women in front" means in practice
The calendar builds this into each event:

| Event | Where women are in front |
|-------|--------------------------|
| Intro to cloud computing (19 Oct) | The ladies help run the beginner session and the cloud study group |
| Cybersecurity basics (26 Oct) | The demos are done by the women in the club |
| Data science session (9 Nov) | Each group has a woman as team lead |
| Build a small web or mobile project (16 Nov) | Women lead the teams |
| Hackathon (23 Nov) | Every team should have women in it |

## Who is it for?
- **Women who are curious about tech.** No experience is needed for most events.
- **Women with experience** who want to lead, demo, mentor or captain a team.
- **Everyone else.** Everyone is welcome to come, learn, support and join the teams.

## Principles
- **Beginner friendly:** every session welcomes people who are new
- **Hands-on:** you try things, you do not only listen
- **Connected to tracks:** what you try on Monday can continue all semester
- **Respectful:** we follow the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/GDG-TUM/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
- **Everyone gets a turn:** teams and demos make space for quieter voices
