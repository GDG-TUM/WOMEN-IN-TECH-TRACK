# 🚀 Getting Started

A short checklist to get ready for your first Women in Tech Monday. It takes about 15 minutes.

## 1. Pick your Mondays
- [ ] Look at the [calendar](calendar/README.md) and choose the events that interest you
- [ ] Not sure where to start? Come to the **kickoff on Monday 12 October**, where we go through every track

## 2. Find out the time and venue
- [ ] The time and venue for each Monday are **announced before the day**. Watch the channel and the [events](events/README.md) pages.
- [ ] Dates may shift a little if the university changes its calendar

## 3. Bring what you need
Most events need only yourself. For some, bring (suggested, to be confirmed on each event page):
- A **phone** for the cybersecurity basics demos (26 Oct)
- A **laptop** for the AI and ML, data science and project sessions (2, 9 and 16 Nov)

No laptop? Tell the organisers. Pair up with someone or ask about options.

## 4. Join the community
- [ ] Join the group channel (link shared by the organisers)
- [ ] [Introduce yourself](members/README.md) on GitHub (optional, and a gentle first step into GitHub)
- [ ] Read the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/GDG-TUM/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## 5. Want a bigger role?
- [ ] Want to lead a demo? See the [guide](get-involved/lead-a-demo.md)
- [ ] Want to lead a team? See the [guide](get-involved/team-lead-guide.md)
- [ ] Send the [volunteer form](https://github.com/GDG-TUM/women-in-tech/issues/new/choose)

## ✅ Ready?
See the [kickoff page](events/2026-10-12-kickoff.md).
