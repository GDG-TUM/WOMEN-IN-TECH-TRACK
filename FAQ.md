# ❓ FAQ

### Who can come?
**Everyone is welcome.** The series is built so that women are in front: leading demos, leading teams and helping run sessions.

### Do I need experience?
No. Most events are beginner friendly. The cloud session is a beginner session, and the AI and ML session says no experience needed. Teams are mixed, so you can learn from each other.

### When and where are the events?
Every Monday of Semester 1, from 12 October to 23 November. The **time and venue are announced before each Monday**, and dates may shift if the university changes its calendar.

### What should I bring?
Usually a phone or laptop, depending on the event. Each [event page](events/README.md) says what to bring, and the organisers confirm it before the day.

### I do not have a laptop. Can I still take part?
Tell the organisers. You can pair up with someone, and we will look for options.

### What if I miss a Monday?
Each event page has a recap and resources. The linked track also keeps its materials online, so you can catch up and join in later.

### How do I get involved beyond attending?
Lead a [demo](get-involved/lead-a-demo.md), lead a [team](get-involved/team-lead-guide.md), help host the cloud study group, or use the [volunteer form](https://github.com/GDG-TUM/women-in-tech/issues/new/choose).

### Is it only for Computer Science students?
No. Students from any course are welcome. The design and data events especially benefit from different backgrounds.

### How do I contact someone?
Open a [question issue](https://github.com/GDG-TUM/women-in-tech/issues/new/choose), or email [gdgtum@gmail.com](mailto:gdgtum@gmail.com).

### What is GitHub for in all this?
This repo keeps the calendar, event pages and recaps in one place. You do not need to use GitHub to attend. If you want to learn it, [introduce yourself](members/README.md) as a first step.
